Types of Earrings

Explore different styles of earrings and discover their unique designs.

Stud Earrings

Stud earrings are simple and elegant. They sit directly on the earlobe and are perfect for everyday wear.

Hoop Earrings

Hoop earrings are circular or semi-circular earrings that create a classic and fashionable look.

Dangler Earrings

Dangler earrings hang below the earlobe and often feature beautiful shapes, stones and decorations.

Jhumka Earrings

Jhumkas are traditional Indian earrings known for their beautiful bell-shaped design.

Chandelier Earrings

Chandelier earrings have elaborate designs that hang below the ear. They are often worn for special occasions.

Ear Cuffs

Ear cuffs wrap around the edge of the ear and provide a modern, stylish appearance.
